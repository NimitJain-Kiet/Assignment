About the Organization

IBM

International Business Machines Corporation (IBM) is a multinational technology company that works in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Cybersecurity, and Machine Learning. IBM has contributed significantly to the development and adoption of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technologies. Its AI platform, IBM watsonx, provides tools and solutions that organizations can use to develop, train, and deploy AI and Machine Learning models. IBM also provides educational resources that help students and professionals understand Machine Learning concepts and their applications in the real world.

Role of IBM in Machine Learning
IBM uses Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to develop solutions for areas such as:

  • Business analytics
  • Healthcare
  • Financial services
  • Customer service
  • Cybersecurity
  • Automation
  • Data analysis
IBM is therefore a suitable organization to study when learning about the practical applications of Machine Learning in modern businesses.
