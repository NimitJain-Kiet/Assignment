My Favorite Place - Dharamshala

Important: Dharamsala is a town in the Indian state of Himachal Pradesh.
Famous Attraction:

  • The spiritual Tsuglagkhang Complex (Dalai Lama's temple)
  • The scenic Triund Hill trek
  • The picturesque HPCA Cricket Stadium
  • Bhagsu Waterfall
  • The historic Kangra Fort

About the Place

The town is located in the Kangra Valley, in the shadow of the Dhauladhar range of the Himalayas at an altitude of 1,457 metres (4,780 ft). Upper Dharamshala, located at an elevation of approximately 1,830 metres, is home to the official residence and headquarters of the 14th Dalai Lama. This area, which includes the well-known suburbs of McLeod Ganj and Forsyth Ganj, still reflects a distinctly colonial character, echoing its British-era legacy. In contrast, Lower Dharamshala, situated at around 1,380 metres, has evolved into a bustling commercial hub, serving as the town's primary centre for trade and business.[10] The economy of the region is highly dependent on agriculture and tourism. The town is now a major hill station and spiritual centre. As of 2024, Dharamshala is the second most populous city in Himachal Pradesh, with a population of approximately 53,543, ranking only after the state capital, Shimla.

Key Location Details
    State: Himachal Pradesh, India
    Region: Kangra Valley, Western Himalayas
    Elevation: About 1,457 meters (4,780 feet)
    Distances: 18 km from Kangra town, 240 km from Shimla, and roughly 490 km from New Delhi

Travel and Access
    Air: Gaggal Airport (Kangra Airport) is about 23 km away.
    Rail: Pathankot railway station is about 85 km away.
    Road: Well connected via scenic highways from Delhi and Chandigarh.
